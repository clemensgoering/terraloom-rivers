# Physical fall publication gate (13 October 2026)

An explicit, single-site fall on one already bound Core-owned Terrain tile now moves from `FallCandidateOnly` to a complete plan only after `PreparedFallEarthworks` has prepared and checked its terrain hole, closed rock solid, collider contact, upper/fall/lower water meshes, pool and outlet. The published plan carries a hard protected footprint. The ordinary river route and format-2 JSON path remain unchanged; an owned fall uses format 3. A fall on supplied terrain, a prepared external source or a multi-tile/brush site is still rejected with a diagnostic.

`TerraLoomRivers.PrepareFromSnapshot` stages terrain, collider-backed rock, water, plan and JSON without changing the live world. `PreparedPublication` validates the old bindings immediately before swapping them. Disposal before completion restores the previous terrain, collider, root, plan and JSON. `LoadPlanJson` validates against the staged complete plan before binding anything. The Rivers inspector exposes fall rock and optional fall-water materials; baking accepts a published fall without irrelevant bed/bank materials and persists the fall materials.

Evidence: `Temp/FallPublication-20261013-c.xml` (Unity 6000.3.9f1 EditMode, 1/1) exercises complete publication, protected reservation, mesh/collider and TerrainData binding, aborted staging, stale live binding, invalid JSON, valid JSON reload, cancellation, repeat generation and Clear. `Tools/RiverPlannerTests.csproj` passes 21/21 portable checks; `Tools/verify.py --compile --development-core ../Core` passes without warnings/errors. Earlier earthworks native test: `Temp/FallEarthworksGate-20261013.xml` (1/1).

The separate-process bake/reopen gate now passes: `RiverFallPersistenceTests.BuildBakedFallFixture` saves a scene with persisted original/carved TerrainData, meshes and materials, and a second Unity process runs `ReopenBakedFallAndValidateJson`. Both report 1/1 in `.artifacts/FallGate4-20261013/{build,reopen}.xml`. Reopen checks Terrain and TerrainCollider bindings, the hole, four rendered fall parts, the rock collider, the original source asset and format-3 JSON reload. The fixture is regenerated under ignored `Assets/TerraLoom/Tests/Generated/` and is not a shipped demo scene.

The Windows Development Player build passes 1/1 (`Temp/FallPlayerBuild.xml`) from the baked scene. Its opt-in `RiverFallPlayerProbe` exits 0 with three downward support raycasts (lip hits rock, pool/outlet hit TerrainCollider) and captures fixed 960×540 views at `.artifacts/FallGate4-20261013/images/{lip,side,foot,pool,outlet}.png`; see `Player.log`. This proves a narrow runtime contact/build gate only. Visual inspection **fails** buyer presentation: the fall wall reads as a vertical plate, its foot has a conspicuous transition against untextured terrain, pool/outlet water appears as a simple flat polygon and the outlet ends abruptly. The visual references are a bare test fixture with no terrain layers or dressing, but the shape/termination defects cannot be attributed to missing art alone. A capsule/walking player, rotated and near-vertical variants and same-camera Epos comparison were not tested here.

This is a bounded physical site release, not general waterfall readiness. Automatic fall discovery, foreign terrain and multi-tile falls remain unsupported. A single route-bound fall cannot currently be composed with other river routes in the same request batch.

Core editor files are concurrently edited and were not changed in this gate.
