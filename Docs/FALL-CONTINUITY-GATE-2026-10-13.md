# Fall continuation contract gate (13 October 2026)

The owned fall is a validated **local physical site**, not a continuous source-to-mouth river. Gate4 Player views show the lower water strip floating above the descending source terrain beyond the carved pool/outlet, then ending as a polygon at the mouth. The wall's plate-like shoulders and the hard foot seam are separate geometry defects; bare test materials make them more visible but do not cause the missing receiving route.

The code explains the discontinuity. `RiverFallCandidatePlanner` records `UpperStart` and `LowerEnd` but builds `WaterfallRecipe` with both reach slopes zero. `WaterfallWaterGeometry` samples the lower strip to `LowerEnd`, whereas `PreparedFallEarthworks.CarvePool` cuts terrain only through `OutletStation + OutletTransitionLength`. `RiverPlan.WithPreparedFall` promotes exactly one candidate with zero `RiverRoute`s. The exported contribution holds a hard protected footprint, but no lower `WaterCorridor` or crossing offers. Extending the mesh or changing its slope cannot create those missing planned, carved and reservable reaches.

`RiverPlan.HasContinuousSourceToMouth` now distinguishes ordinary complete routes from a locally published fall. `RequireContinuousSourceToMouth` raises a precise missing-contract diagnostic. Composition invokes this before it publishes shared Rivers/Paths planning, preserving the previous world when a fall request is present. Standalone owned fall generation, bake and reopen remain available as a local geometry preview; its hard reservation still prohibits an unreviewed crossing. This does not loosen a site, slope, reservation, terrain ownership or publication guard.

Gate6 should produce a single planned fall + receiving `RiverRoute` up to a defined mouth/waterbody or world boundary. The plan needs sampled descending water/bed/banks, stable identity, budgets and reservations; a lower `WaterCorridor` and explicit crossing offers; excavation/texture treatment over the same full reach; a validated join from pool sill to route geometry and collider-backed banks; and one prepared commit for terrain, fall, route, plans and JSON. A straight-only first version is acceptable only if the same runtime planner verifies the route and rejects unsupported terrain rather than displaying a loose strip. The same design must eventually include the upper reach and rotated sites. Do not infer support for foreign terrain or multiple tiles from the current owned, axis-aligned fixture.

Portable RiverPlanner checks assert ordinary route continuity and fall refusal. Native `RiverFallPublicationTests` asserts that the standalone published fall remains locally complete but fails the full-reach guard. Gate4 bake/reopen and Player images are retained as an honest baseline; the visual acceptance is still failed.

Later boundary-only route publication is recorded in `BOUNDARY-FALL-PUBLICATION-2026-10-13.md`. The guard still rejects legacy local falls and Composition candidate-only planning; a physically published boundary fall can now satisfy `HasContinuousSourceToMouth` in standalone Rivers.
