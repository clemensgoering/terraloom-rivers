# Waterfall reach draft — not implemented

TerraLoom Rivers owns this ability; no additional mandatory product module.
Source requirements: Epos `_Docs/Konzept-Weltaufbau-nach-Regeln-2026-10-06.md`,
section “Wasserfälle: gemeinsamer Verbraucherbedarf – 10.10.2026”. Epos remains
the production consumer and later a read-only module pilot. Clemens explicitly
asks Epos for its OWN design; compare both proposals and combine the strongest
parts. This does not authorize TerraLoom to modify Epos production files.

## TerraLoom's proposal to compare

Use an explicit piecewise longitudinal profile: ordinary upper reach, rounded
lip, controlled cubic falling strip, rounded foot into a narrow elongated pool,
then an outlet neck into the ordinary downstream reach. Constrain drop curves
to descend and bind their endpoints/tangents to the two water levels. Sample
arc length for continuous UV flow; never globally stretch texture speed with
fall height. Use a large original rock mass behind the strip, asymmetric shoulder
rocks and measured wet-contact bands. Some sections may detach from rock, but
only a deliberate planned free-fall section may have an air gap. Plain default
water strips, no compulsory purchased assets, and effects disabled for first
contact evidence. Split a cascade into explicit smaller drops sharing the same
pool/outlet plan rather than independently perturbing vertices.

Epos's independently read proposal uses guided rock-contact channels and a
Top/Middle/Base asset adapter. Compare on the SAME profile and cameras, without
mist first: lip closure, side rock contact, computed impact anchor, pool outlet,
UV scale, navigation and measured cost. Generic anchor/profile contracts can
support both. Keep purchased kit files in Epos; transfer no assets. Epos trial
2–4m fall /1.2–1.5 pool-width ratio remains an evaluation range, not a standard.
No variant is currently built or accepted. See the source's additional section
“Wasserfälle: eigener Epos-Entwurf und Vergleichsbasis – 10.10.2026”.

## One shared plan

Represent normal reach, cascade, lip/drop, impact pool and outflow explicitly.
A deterministic fall identity connects upstream water level, rock lip, drop height,
impact footprint, pool floor/water level and visible downstream outlet. The small
pool is only somewhat wider than the river; width/depth/rock shape remain style
settings. Classification thresholds and pool ratios are NOT approved yet.
Never stretch one horizontal water mesh over different upper/lower levels.

Heightfields form stepped valley floor, pool and outflow. Vertical/undercut walls
need supplementary rock geometry; baked final terrain remains physical truth.
Rendering, collider, wet/danger areas and navigation consume the same plan and
revision. First solve lip/contact/continuous flow and rock silhouette; foam,
spray, sound and underwater decoration are optional later visual participants.

## Order and rights

1. Capture seed/manual intent, recipe and source revision.
2. Plan all reaches, lip, impact and pool/outflow; reserve their exact local
   rights and fall-clearance volumes before Paths chooses routes/crossings.
3. Plan Paths, then stage authorized terrain edits and an immutable final sampler.
4. Revalidate crossings, safe shore access and all water/rock/ground contacts
   against that same staged result. No independent renderer height adjustments.
5. Publish terrain, water and physical blockers consistently through the proposed
   shared composition boundary. This boundary is an ADR, not current atomic code.

Protected landmarks, coast and other reaches retain rights. Permission to one
fall does not open its enclosing rectangle or neighboring water. Paths cannot
cross the rock wall, water jet or required empty fall volume. Deep wet and steep
areas close agent navigation; dry authorized shore routes stay passable.

A tile-crossing fall is planned once with stable owner ID and shared boundary
samples. Neighbor tiles must not duplicate pool/lip. Cancellation/unload cannot
leave half a published fall. Optional VFX may load by distance with hysteresis;
physical state, distant silhouette and continuous outlet cannot disappear.

## First implementation gate

One controlled fixed-seed step with small pool and visible outlet, plus the same
manual intent. Negative fixtures: tile seam, protected neighboring reach, unsafe
crossing/agent route, cancellation and stale revision. Check monotone ordinary
reaches, lip closure, pool/outlet contact, final heights and clearance. Capture
player height, side of the step, along outflow and a measured cross-section with
terrain/water/deepest geometry and 1.8m reference. Record timings and numeric
results separately from visual judgment. Do not start with a global hydrology
rewrite or promise this draft is already working.

The actual relief reference was viewed: irregular mountain/rock bands, detailed
surfaces and dense valley vegetation. It is not a low-poly art goal and does not
itself demonstrate a waterfall. No Epos product files or purchased art are copied.
