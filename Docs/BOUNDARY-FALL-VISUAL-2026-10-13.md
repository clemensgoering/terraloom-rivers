# Gate6g: paired fall/bridge visual probe

This gate keeps the same 32 m Core-owned fall/receiving-route/TimberTruss Composition scene, fixed camera transforms, one directional-light setup and 960×540 Windows Player capture. The ten bare-fixture images are preserved in `Integration/.artifacts/BoundaryFallBridgePlayer/bare-images/`. Images after original procedural materials but before rock shaping are in `material-only-images/`; the current images are in `images/`. Compare `fall-wide`, `foot`, `receiving-bank`, `bridge-end`, `bridge-underside` and `overview` across these folders. The initial five Gate6c camera names remain available, although `lip`, `side` and `outlet` are partly obscured by cliff or bridge in the Composition scene.

`BoundaryFallVisualKit` produces all fixture textures, TerrainLayers, materials and simple mesh props algorithmically in Unity Editor code; there are no external texture/model sources. It uses stable seeded pixels for soil, grass, stone and timber, tinted transparent water and URP Lit materials. The 128×128 Terrain alphamap distinguishes upper green ground, the cliff band and dry receiving banks. It also creates original low-poly trunk/crown/stone meshes, three green summer trees, three pale winter trees and four stones. These are visual fixture props placed after planning without colliders; they do **not** demonstrate `RegionDecoration` rules or a ready-to-ship product asset library. The generated assets are local to `Integration/Assets/TerraLoom/Integration/Generated/BoundaryFallBridge/VisualKit/`; their source generator is tracked under `Rivers/IntegrationProject/Assets/TerraLoom/Integration/Editor/`.

`PreparedFallEarthworks.BuildSolid` now subdivides the front wall vertically, adds deterministic recessed relief limited to 8 cm beneath the water-contact strip and up to 30 cm on exposed shoulders, and gives the mesh UVs for rock texture. A closed eight-row toe connects the lower wall to the carved terrain with a curved height profile. Side and bottom faces use matching rows so there are no T-junction/open edges. The physical water-wall clearance, full-width bed/collider contact, source immutability and publication rollback remain mandatory. Unity `RiverFallEarthworksSiteTests` passes 2/2 in `Rivers/Temp/Gate6gToe1.xml`; `RiverFallPublicationTests` passes 2/2 in `Rivers/Temp/Gate6gPublicationFinal.xml`. The final shared Bake and separate-process Reopen pass 1/1 each in `Integration/Temp/BoundaryFallPropsBake1.xml` and `BoundaryFallPropsReopen1.xml`. Windows Player build and run exit 0; `Integration/.artifacts/BoundaryFallBridgePlayer/PropsPlayer1.log` retains 61 continuous bridge steps, three landings and ten camera captures.

Visual finding from the images: material separation reveals the channel and the timber construction, and the front rock shoulders are no longer perfectly planar. **V0 still fails.** The source terrain fixture itself has a tile-wide abrupt plateau edge, so the fall reads as a straight artificial wall. The wetted center remains nearly planar under the strict contact limit, and the visible rock/water/terrain foot junction is still sharp. The curved toe changed the closed collider surface but made little visible difference from the fixed foot camera. The procedural tree/rock props are intentionally simple placeholders and do not establish buyer-grade art quality. A natural source terrain recipe, a shaped cliff-to-terrain shoulder, pool foam/wetness and terrain-aware shoreline detail are still required before reference parity or marketing acceptance. No Epos product files were modified.
