# Prepared fall earthworks: owned-tile staging gate

`RiverFallEarthworksSite.Inspect` and `PreparedFallEarthworks.Prepare` are opt-in staging APIs for one explicit, axis-aligned fall on one currently bound Core-owned TerrainData. They consume a validated route-bound candidate and exact Core `PlanSnapshot`. The preflight checks the site identity, whole owned height grid, hole-grid bounds, full pool/outlet space and pre-existing holes before changing anything. A changed height value, stale revision, foreign terrain, occupied hole or cancellation fails before publication.

Preparation clones TerrainData, carves a compact lower pool and outlet bed, opens only the candidate's aligned hole, builds a welded-edge-closed wall/ground solid with MeshCollider-compatible triangles, and uses the candidate's `WaterfallRecipe` for the three continuous water meshes. Temporary native TerrainCollider and MeshCollider probes test lip support and the vertical wall across the water width plus the actual pool/outlet bed along and across the reach. Failed preparation disposes cloned terrain, meshes and temporary probe objects; the original TerrainData is unchanged.

This document records the earlier staging-only gate. Publication was added later in `FALL-PUBLICATION-GATE-2026-10-13.md`; the receiving-reach contract and failed Player visuals are assessed in `FALL-CONTINUITY-GATE-2026-10-13.md`. No rotated fall, foreign terrain or multi-tile claim follows from either gate.
