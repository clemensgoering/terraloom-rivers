# Gate6a: planned straight receiving reach

`RiverFallSite(requirePlannedLowerReach: true)` opt-ins one fall request to a read-only lower-route contract. `RiverFallLowerReach` starts at the exact end station and water height of the existing pool/outlet transition, samples to the named mouth at bounded spacing, and carries the same Core input and `RiverPlan.Identity` as the candidate. Its water and bed must descend within `MaximumSlope`; each centre station must have a cuttable bed and both banks must support water across the full configured width. Missing support, an excessive grade, protected terrain, bounds escape or a second simultaneous request rejects the plan. Ordinary routes and the legacy local fall site retain their previous fingerprint and behavior; the opt-in adds `fall-lower-route-v1` to the domain fingerprint.

Each validated span has a stable `WaterCorridor`, hard bed and soft bank reservations. Crossing offers are **Bridge only**, and only where the whole candidate footprint lies at least one channel width beyond the outlet transition. The proposed route, reservations and offers remain attached to the *candidate* for inspection; they are not yet included in `RiverPlan.Routes`, `ToContribution`, terrain carving, JSON or Unity publication. The current hard fall reservation still denies actual crossings. `HasContinuousSourceToMouth` remains false and Composition still fails closed.

This intentional separation keeps Gate4 bake/reopen and the existing Player images unchanged. In particular, the lower-plan slope does not alter `WaterfallRecipe` or render a disconnected slope before its matching terrain earthworks exist. Gate6b must carve and render the planned reach, match the pool join and mouth boundary, add a true published route/offer contribution under the same identity, then atomically publish and validate it in a new scene/Player. A named mouth still needs a physical receiving waterbody, boundary or downstream continuation policy; an arbitrary interior terrain point cannot make water vanish convincingly. Rotated and supplied-terrain sites remain unsupported.

Portable `Tools/RiverPlannerTests.csproj` checks join/end stations, common identity, aligned water/bed/banks, downhill shape, deterministic replay, bounded bridge-only offers, and rejection for a weak downstream bank or steep grade. These are planning checks, not a visual or physics pass.

Later owned boundary earthworks and standalone publication are documented in `BOUNDARY-RECEIVING-EARTHWORKS-2026-10-13.md` and `BOUNDARY-FALL-PUBLICATION-2026-10-13.md`. This document remains the Gate6a plan-only record.
