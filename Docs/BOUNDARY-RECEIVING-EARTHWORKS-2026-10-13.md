# Gate6b: prepared owned-tile receiving earthworks

The optional runtime `RiverFallSite(..., requirePlannedLowerReach: true, worldBoundaryMouth: true)` accepts only an axis-aligned downstream mouth exactly at the edge of a finite height grid. Its plan identity includes the boundary policy. Candidate corridors are clipped at that edge and no bridge offer touches it. Interior mouths, absent grids, unsupported grades/banks, protected areas and multiple simultaneous fall requests still reject. This is an explicit **world-boundary outlet**, not a generic interior sink or waterbody integration.

`PreparedFallEarthworks.Prepare` now stages, without binding live terrain, an owned TerrainData clone with a cut-only receiving channel from the pool-transition join through the world edge. It keeps full pool depth at that join, produces three original fall/pool meshes only to the join plus a fourth water strip following the planned lower water stations, and checks the shared mesh seam. Temporary terrain/rock colliders verify the existing lip, wall, pool/sill and receiving bed along and across the entire strip. At the exact terrain edge, where a Unity TerrainCollider ray may not hit, it checks an inward collider point and the TerrainData height at the edge across the water width. The original terrain and root remain unchanged until a later publication gate.

`TerraLoomRivers.PrepareFallPublication` deliberately rejects this boundary-mouth variant as **staging-only**. No plan promotion, contribution, JSON, bake, scene or Player claim follows yet. Gate6c must install four water meshes, publish the receiving corridor/reservations/limited crossing offers, shorten the local hard fall footprint to the lip/pool, version the saved plan and commit terrain/root/plan/collider atomically. Only then may `HasContinuousSourceToMouth` become true. It must also validate rollback and a baked reopen before Player/visual acceptance; the previous Gate4 screenshots remain an honest failure baseline.

Evidence: portable RiverPlanner checks 23/23; native `RiverFallEarthworksSiteTests` 2/2 in `Temp/Gate6bEarthworks-20261013-final.xml` (boundary staging plus prior legacy/stale/foreign/hole checks); legacy publication 1/1 in `Temp/Gate6bLegacy-20261013.xml`. The boundary test verifies four water meshes, source immutability, cut depth at the world edge, no live publication, cancellation and stale source rejection.
