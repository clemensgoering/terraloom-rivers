# Gate6h: bounded source-terrain shoulder

The existing boundary-fall Composition fixture used a tile-wide abrupt source height step at x=6 m. Gate6h changes only the **Bake-only Core-owned source TerrainData recipe** in `BoundaryFallCompositionTests`: at the center of the fall the lip remains sharp, while lateral distance broadens the height transition into a smooth shoulder. The generator, fall/receiving plan, water/solid meshes, colliders, materials, seed 42, scene path and ten Player cameras are unchanged. The non-Bake functional test retains the old fixture so unrelated baseline tests do not silently change.

The fixed scene now reads as one fall cut into a high bank with sloping outer shoulders rather than a tile-wide vertical wall. This establishes that the large flat cliff came from the source fixture, not from the Rivers solid. The current foot camera still shows a hard water/pool seam and a dark rock/terrain junction. The receiving bank and bridge remain continuous but look schematic. **Visual V0 remains failed**; this is a diagnostic improvement, not a market-ready waterfall.

Unity 6000.3.9f1 Bake passed 1/1 and separate-process Reopen passed 1/1. The Windows D3D11 Player build and run exited 0; the probe passed 61 continuous deck steps, three landings and ten fixed-camera captures. The evidence is isolated at `Integration/.artifacts/Gate6h-20261013-rivers-3b8fde3-fixture-a3b2fd6a/`. Its `manifest.json` records the full Core/Paths/Rivers revisions, dirty fixture SHA-256, Player hash, seed and camera settings. `Bake.xml`, `Reopen.xml`, `Player.log`, and the images in that folder belong to this run. Compare `images/fall-wide.png`, `images/foot.png`, `images/receiving-bank.png`, and `images/overview.png` with the earlier Gate6g images. Gate6g's original `images` path has been reused by later probes and should not be cited as immutable evidence.

Contact boundaries: the existing Bake test exercises Rivers' solid/water/terrain contact validation and shared Composition publication. The source-only change passes these checks, so the tile-wide wall was fixture-caused. The remaining foot seam cannot be attributed to the source height alone: the water fall and pool meet at exactly coincident mesh vertices, but their geometries form a sharp transition. A separate normal-only trial passed technical checks yet produced no meaningful visual improvement, and was reverted. A true contact-preserving impact transition needs its own bounded design and Player gate.

Rotated/near-vertical scope: the pure `WaterfallFallSurfaceTests` already cover zero horizontal run, 2.5 cm run, and a rotated X-direction frame (6/6 on this session's focused run). This does not validate a rotated **Composition bridge**: `RiverFallLowerReach` currently supplies no axis-aligned crossing offer for a diagonal fall. Gate6h makes no claim for diagonal bridge publication, and no constraint was relaxed. No Epos files were changed.
